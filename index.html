    // why it is different

    Built to be right about you, not to send the most applications.

    Most tools either rewrite your résumé to please a keyword filter or blast out hundreds of applications in your name. NextBeacon does neither. It helps you choose well, then helps you apply well.

    You press Submit. Always.

    The Apply button opens the employer's own page with your résumé ready. You read it, you decide, and you send it. Nothing goes out in your name without you, so nothing can embarrass you.

    • NeverApplies to a job for you, in bulk or one at a time
    • NeverSends a résumé or a letter you have not read
    • NeverContacts an employer or a recruiter on your behalf

    A fact check on every résumé.

    Before a résumé is saved, every number, employer, job title, degree and certification is checked word for word against your own CV. A claim that is not in your CV does not get through. Your reputation took twenty years to build; this protects it.

    • Led a 40-person program teamFound in your CV

    It scores you, not keywords.

    Each posting gets a 0 to 5 fit score against your actual experience, pay floor, level, location and the work you want, with the reasons written out. 4.0 and up means apply. 3.5 to 3.9 means worth a look.

    Private by design.

    NextBeacon runs on your own PC with your own Claude account. There is no NextBeacon server, and nothing of yours is stored by us. If you are searching while employed, nobody can tell.

    // questions

    The things people ask before they buy.

    Is this another auto-apply tool?

    No. NextBeacon never applies to a job for you, in bulk or one at a time. It finds and scores postings and prepares your résumé. You read it, you open the employer's page, and you press Submit. Nothing is ever sent in your name without you.

    Will the AI make things up on my résumé?

    It is built not to. Before a résumé is saved, every number, employer, job title, degree and certification is checked word for word against your own CV, and anything that is not there is removed. AI can still make mistakes, so read every résumé and letter before you send it. You always have the final say.

    I am not technical. Can I set this up?

    Yes. You run one installer. It checks that your PC is ready, installs everything it needs, and opens a page-by-page setup that asks plain questions: your résumé, the roles you want, your pay floor, remote or on-site, and the companies to watch. There is no command window and no code. Most people are done in about fifteen minutes.

    Where does my information go?

    It stays on your PC. Your résumé, your profile and your job history live in files on your own computer. There is no NextBeacon server, and nothing of yours is stored by us. The AI work happens through your own Claude account, under your own name, so posting text and parts of your profile go to Anthropic and nowhere else. The full details are on the privacy page.

    What is Claude, and why do I need an account?

    Claude is the AI from Anthropic that reads postings, scores them and drafts your résumés. NextBeacon uses it through an account that belongs to you, so your information is never pooled with anyone else's. You pay Anthropic for that plan directly, about $20 a month for Claude Pro. If your plan lapses, the AI features pause until you renew it.

    Is there a subscription?

    Not to NextBeacon. You pay $299 once and it is yours to keep on one Windows PC. The only running cost is your own Claude plan.

    Does it work on a Mac?

    Not yet. NextBeacon runs on Windows 10 and 11. If you only have a Mac, please do not buy it yet.

    Can it read every company's job board?

    Most of them. NextBeacon reads the job boards that companies publish on, and it finds a company's board when you type the company's name. A few large employers block automated reading of their career pages, and those cannot be scanned. You can still add them and check them by hand.

    Does it promise interviews or offers?

    No, and nobody honestly can. NextBeacon helps you choose well and apply well. A score is an opinion about fit, not a prediction. Whether you get the job depends on you, the employer and the market.

    What if it is not for me?

    Ask within 7 days of your purchase and you get a full refund. The refund ends the licence, so the copy stops working. The terms have the details.
