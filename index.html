    // why it is different

    Built to be right about you, not to send the most applications.

    Most tools either rewrite your résumé to please a keyword filter or blast out hundreds of applications on your behalf. RoleRadar does neither. It helps you choose well, then helps you apply well.

    It scores you, not keywords.

    Each posting gets a 0 to 5 fit score against your actual experience, pay floor, level, location and the work you want, with the reasons written out. 4.0 and up means apply. 3.5 to 3.9 means worth a look.

    4.2
    of 5
    • Program leadership matches your last two roles
    • Remote, as you asked
    • Pay is above your floor

    A fact check on every résumé.

    Before a résumé is saved, every number, employer, job title, degree and certification is checked word for word against your own CV. A claim that is not in your CV does not get through.

    • Led a 40-person program teamFound in your CV
    • Cut schedule risk by 22%Found in your CV
    • Managed 50 participantsNot in your CV · removed

    Straight from the source.

    It reads the career pages employers themselves publish, for the companies you pick, and drops postings that have since closed. No recycled listings.

    Private by design.

    RoleRadar runs on your own PC with your own Claude account. There is no RoleRadar server, and nothing of yours is stored by us.

    You stay in control.

    The Apply button opens the employer's own page with your résumé ready. You review it and you press Submit. Nothing is sent in your name without you.

    // how it works

    From double-click to your first scored list.

    Install

    Unzip the package and double-click one file. A short window installs everything it needs.

    Tell it about you

    Upload your résumé, then answer a few plain questions: roles, pay, remote or on-site, companies.

    Let it scan

    Each morning it reads new postings, scores them, and emails you the ones worth a look.

    Apply with confidence

    Open the tailored résumé and the employer's page, review, and press Submit yourself.

    // good to know

    What you need, and what to expect.

    What you need

    • A Windows 10 or 11 PC. Mac is not supported yet.
    • A paid Claude plan, on the account you sign in with.
    • About fifteen minutes for the guided install, mostly waiting.
    • Your résumé, as a PDF, Word file or text.

    What to expect

    • RoleRadar scores and drafts. You decide and you apply.
    • AI can make mistakes. Read every résumé and letter before you send it.
    • A few large employers block automated reading of their career pages, so those cannot be scanned.
    • No one can promise interviews or offers, and RoleRadar does not.
