Looking up your purchase…

  1. Check your emailA message from info@mynextbeacon.com carries the download link for the installer, your purchase key and the setup steps. It usually arrives within a minute or two; check your spam folder if it has not.
  2. Download and run the installerThe installer shows the terms, checks that your PC meets the requirements, then asks for your purchase key. If a check fails, it tells you what to change and you can run it again.
  3. Walk through the first setupAbout fifteen minutes: sign in to your Claude account, add your résumé, say what you are looking for, pick your companies, set the morning email and the daily time.
  4. Start your first scanFrom then on NextBeacon runs every morning on its own. You press Submit.
