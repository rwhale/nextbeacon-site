How we scored

Each tool was read from its own site and its own page on Trustpilot on 30 October 2026, with a second check on 31 October. A mark of yes means the tool does the thing as its main way of working; partly means it offers it as an option, a tier or a side feature, or does it in a weaker form; no means it does not, or the recurring complaints on its review page say the opposite. Prices are the published price of the plan that includes the features scored, as shown on that date; most are monthly and renew. Ratings are Trustpilot's score and review count on that date. "Watch out" lines are the most repeated theme in that tool's own one-star reviews, not our opinion.

We did not test the tools against each other on live searches, and we say so. If you run one of these tools and a line here is wrong or out of date, write to info@mynextbeacon.com and we will correct it, with a note of what changed.
